Stovetop math

The pasta water takes forever, the lid sits next to the pot, and the kettle crowd swears the ring is burning money. Stovetop math turns liters and watts into honest minutes to a boil, the lid into the minutes it actually saves, and the weekly habit into kWh and cost per year. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Boil plan - liters, watts and start temperature become minutes and kWh.
  • Lid plan - the same boil with a labeled lid becomes the minutes saved.
  • Year plan - boils a week become kWh and cost per year.

Labeled norms

Labeled physics: 4.186 kJ to raise a liter by one degree, at a labeled 80% ring efficiency. Labeled start: 20°C tap water. Labeled lid effect: about 25% faster with the lid on. Labeled tariff: 0.15 per kWh; put in your own. These are reference values, not rules - altitude, pot and ring decide the real numbers.
